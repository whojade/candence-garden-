Cadence Garden

Type a sentence and press Enter. The plant grows from how you typed it: your speed, your hesitations, the gaps between words. Nobody else types the same sentence the same way, so nobody else grows the same plant.

Bars show the gap before each key. Tall bars become flowers.

How it grows. Each word is a stem segment. Typing faster than your own average bends the stem one way, slower bends it the other. A pause longer than 0.7 s before a word opens a flower there. Overall speed sets the leaf colour, from cool blue-green when you're careful to warm yellow-green when you're flying. Backspaces leave thorns.
